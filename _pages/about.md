I'm a Ph.D. student at the National University of Singapore, in the Programming Languages and Software Engineering (PLSE) lab within the Trustworthy Software Systems group, advised by Professor [Abhik Roychoudhury](https://abhikrc.com). My research sits at the intersection of program analysis, automated program repair, and large language models, with a focus on making LLM-assisted software engineering more reliable and secure — from repairing LLM-generated code to using LLMs to guide fuzzing and fix vulnerabilities found in projects like OSS-Fuzz.

Alongside my PhD, I co-founded [AutoCodeRover](https://www.sonarsource.com/company/press-releases/sonar-acquires-autocoderover-to-supercharge-developers-with-ai-agents/), an NUS spin-off building an autonomous AI agent platform for automated software development, where I served as CTO from 2024 until its acquisition by [Sonar](https://www.sonarsource.com/) in February 2025. I've since continued with Sonar, first as a Research Engineer and now as a Research Consultant, working on agentic program repair and AI-assisted issue resolution.

Before NUS, I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at Eindhoven University of Technology (Cum Laude), and worked on compiler/grammar tooling (ANTLR) and full-stack engineering.

You can find a full list of my papers on the [publications](/publications/) page or on my [Google Scholar profile]({{ site.author.googlescholar }}).
