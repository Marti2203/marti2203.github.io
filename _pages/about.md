I'm a PhD student at the National University of Singapore, where I work in the Programming Languages and Software Engineering (PLSE) Lab within the Trustworthy Software Systems group, advised by Professor [Abhik Roychoudhury](https://abhikrc.com). My research focuses on making LLM-assisted software engineering more reliable and secure through program analysis, automated program repair, fuzzing, and vulnerability remediation.

Research Directions
======

**Automated Program Repair.** Building systems that automatically find and fix bugs in real-world code, with infrastructure for evaluating them fairly.
* [AutoCodeRover: Agentic Program Repair for SonarQube Issues](/publication/2026-07-01-autocoderover-agentic-program-repair-sonarqube) (FSE 2026) — **M. Mirchev**, R. Shariffdeen, H. Ruan, Y. Zhang, A. Roychoudhury
* [Assured Automatic Programming via Large Language Models](/publication/2024-06-01-assured-automatic-programming-via-llms) (arXiv 2024) — **M. Mirchev**, A. Costea, A. K. Singh, A. Roychoudhury

**LLM-Driven Fuzzing & Security.** Using LLMs to understand code and protocol semantics well enough to find and fix real vulnerabilities, while hardening the software supply chains behind AI systems.
* [Fixing Security Vulnerabilities with Agentic AI in OSS-Fuzz](/publication/2026-04-01-fixing-security-vulnerabilities-with-agentic-ai-in-oss-fuzz) (ICSE 2026) — Y. Zhang, J. Wang, D. Berzin, **M. Mirchev**, A. Roychoudhury
* [Large Language Model guided Protocol Fuzzing](/publication/2024-02-26-llm-guided-protocol-fuzzing) (ChatAFL, NDSS 2024) — R. Meng, **M. Mirchev**, M. Böhme, A. Roychoudhury

Alongside my PhD, I co-founded [AutoCodeRover](https://www.sonarsource.com/company/press-releases/sonar-acquires-autocoderover-to-supercharge-developers-with-ai-agents/), an NUS spin-off building an autonomous AI agent platform for automated software development. I served as its CTO from 2024 until its acquisition by [Sonar](https://www.sonarsource.com/) in February 2025. I now continue this work at Sonar as a Research Consultant, after first working there as a Research Engineer, focusing on agentic program repair and AI-assisted issue resolution.

Before NUS, I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at Eindhoven University of Technology (cum laude) and worked on compiler and grammar tooling (ANTLR) as well as full-stack engineering.

You can find a full list of my papers on the [publications](/publications/) page or on my [Google Scholar profile]({{ site.author.googlescholar }}).
