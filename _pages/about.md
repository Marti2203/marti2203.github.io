I'm a Ph.D. student at the National University of Singapore, in the Programming Languages and Software Engineering (PLSE) lab within the Trustworthy Software Systems group, advised by Professor [Abhik Roychoudhury](https://abhikrc.com). My research sits at the intersection of program analysis, automated program repair, and large language models, with a focus on making LLM-assisted software engineering more reliable and secure — from repairing LLM-generated code to using LLMs to guide fuzzing and fix vulnerabilities found in projects like OSS-Fuzz.

Research Directions
======

**Automated Program Repair.** Building systems that automatically fix bugs in real-world code, and the infrastructure to evaluate them fairly.
* [Cerberus](/publication/2023-05-14-cerberus-program-repair-framework), a framework unifying fuzzing, static analysis, and repair tools — R. Shariffdeen, **M. Mirchev**, Y. Noller, A. Roychoudhury
* [Automated Repair of Programs from Large Language Models](/publication/2023-05-14-automated-repair-of-programs-from-large-language-models) — Z. Fan, X. Gao, **M. Mirchev**, A. Roychoudhury, S. H. Tan
* [Assured Automatic Programming via Large Language Models](/publication/2024-06-01-assured-automatic-programming-via-llms) — **M. Mirchev**, A. Costea, A. K. Singh, A. Roychoudhury
* [Computation Tree Logic Guided Program Repair](/publication/2025-06-01-ctl-guided-program-repair) — Y. Liu, Y. Song, **M. Mirchev**, A. Roychoudhury
* [AutoCodeRover](/publication/2026-07-01-autocoderover-agentic-program-repair-sonarqube), agentic repair for SonarQube-flagged issues — **M. Mirchev**, R. Shariffdeen, H. Ruan, Y. Zhang, A. Roychoudhury

**LLM-Driven Fuzzing & Security.** Using LLMs to understand code and protocol semantics well enough to find and fix real vulnerabilities.
* [ChatAFL](/publication/2024-02-26-llm-guided-protocol-fuzzing), LLM-guided protocol fuzzing (NDSS 2024) — R. Meng, **M. Mirchev**, M. Böhme, A. Roychoudhury
* [Fixing Security Vulnerabilities with Agentic AI in OSS-Fuzz](/publication/2026-04-01-fixing-security-vulnerabilities-with-agentic-ai-in-oss-fuzz) — Y. Zhang, J. Wang, D. Berzin, **M. Mirchev**, A. Roychoudhury
* [Detecting Python Malware in the Software Supply Chain with Program Analysis](/publication/2025-04-27-detecting-python-malware-in-the-software-supply-chain) — R. Shariffdeen, B. Hassanshahi, **M. Mirchev**, A. El Husseini, et al.

Alongside my PhD, I co-founded [AutoCodeRover](https://www.sonarsource.com/company/press-releases/sonar-acquires-autocoderover-to-supercharge-developers-with-ai-agents/), an NUS spin-off building an autonomous AI agent platform for automated software development, where I served as CTO from 2024 until its acquisition by [Sonar](https://www.sonarsource.com/) in February 2025. I've since continued with Sonar, first as a Research Engineer and now as a Research Consultant, working on agentic program repair and AI-assisted issue resolution.

Before NUS, I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at Eindhoven University of Technology (Cum Laude), and worked on compiler/grammar tooling (ANTLR) and full-stack engineering.

You can find a full list of my papers on the [publications](/publications/) page or on my [Google Scholar profile]({{ site.author.googlescholar }}).
