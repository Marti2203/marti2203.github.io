[Cerberus](https://github.com/nus-apr/cerberus) is a research platform providing a unified interface to multiple program analysis tools, including fuzzing, static analysis, and automated program repair. The work was presented as a tool demonstration at ICSE 2023 by R. Shariffdeen, **M. Mirchev**, Y. Noller, and A. Roychoudhury; see the [publication](/publication/2023-05-14-cerberus-program-repair-framework) for details.
