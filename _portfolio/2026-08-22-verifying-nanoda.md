Working on formally verifying [nanoda](https://github.com/ammkrn/nanoda_lib), an independent type-checking/proof-checking kernel for the [Lean](https://lean-lang.org/) theorem prover implemented in Rust, using [Verus](https://github.com/verus-lang/verus) to prove correctness properties of its core checking algorithms.
