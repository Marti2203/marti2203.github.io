[ChatAFL](https://github.com/ChatAFLndss/ChatAFL) is a protocol fuzzer guided by large language models, developed for our NDSS 2024 paper by R. Meng, **M. Mirchev**, M. Böhme, and A. Roychoudhury. It uses LLMs to help the fuzzer understand protocol semantics and overcome state-machine coverage plateaus that defeat purely mutation-based fuzzers. See the [publication](/publication/2024-02-26-llm-guided-protocol-fuzzing) for details.
