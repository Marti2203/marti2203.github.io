[ChatAFL](https://github.com/ChatAFLndss/ChatAFL) is a protocol fuzzer guided by large language models, developed for our NDSS 2024 paper. It uses LLMs to help the fuzzer understand protocol semantics and escape state-machine coverage plateaus that purely mutation-based fuzzers get stuck on. See the [publication](/publication/2024-02-26-llm-guided-protocol-fuzzing) for details.
